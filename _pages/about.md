I am an Associate Professor (tenured) at [SANKEN, Osaka University](https://www.sanken.osaka-u.ac.jp/en/). I also hold a cross-appointment at the [Data Science and AI Innovation Research Promotion Center, Shiga University](https://www.ds.shiga-u.ac.jp/dscenter/). In addition, I am a Visiting Scientist at the [Causal Inference Team, RIKEN AIP](https://aip.riken.jp/labs/generic_tech/cause_infer/), and a Visiting Researcher at [Fukuma Research Group, Kyoto University Graduate School of Medicine](https://fukuma-lab.hs.med.kyoto-u.ac.jp/). 

My current research focuses on causal discovery methods that are robust in real-world settings, particularly those that can handle hidden variables and capture complex data-generating mechanisms.

I supervise students at both [Osaka University](https://www.eng.osaka-u.ac.jp/en/) and [Shiga University](https://www.ds.shiga-u.ac.jp/graduate/). See my lab's [page]({{ '/lab' | relative_url }}).
